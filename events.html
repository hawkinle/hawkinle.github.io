What's On

Events and goings-on at the Upper Horton Sports Club.

Every week: the bar is open Wednesday to Sunday from 5:00 PM (Sunday from 12:00 PM), with meals on Friday nights (plus the Friday raffle trays), Sunday lunch from 12 and Sunday pizzas from 5. There's no set closing time — we stay open while there's a crowd, so how late we run depends on patronage.

Melbourne Cup Calcutta

Tickets and lunch from 12 noon, draw from 3 PM with auction to follow. Hot & cold share platters, chicken burger sliders and loaded hotdogs. Get your syndicates together — Cup Day itself is Tuesday 3 November.
