Behind the Pits

Wondering why the barbecue tastes extraordinary? Competitors must abide by strict Australasian Barbecue Alliance (ABA) standards:

60-Point Scoring Matrix

  • Taste (30 Pts): Depth of woodsmoke and meat flavor
  • Texture (20 Pts): Tender bite without turning to mush
  • Appearance (10 Pts): Mahogany bark & natural color

The Golden Rules

  • Zero Garnish: No greens or foreign items in turn-in boxes.
  • Wood/Charcoal Only: Electric/gas for ignition only.
  • Judges Final: 100% blind tasting by certified panel.
