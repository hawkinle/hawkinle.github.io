How the day runs

Meals start at 12:00 and syndicate tickets are available from then, right through until the auction. The auction gets going from about 3:00 PM. Come for lunch, grab your syndicate, stay for the bidding, and stick around to see whose horse gets up.

Syndicates

Nobody wins a Calcutta on their own. Round up your mates, your family, the bloke you drink with on a Friday — get your syndicates together.

Syndicate tickets are available from 12:00, ahead of the auction, so you can sort your crew out over lunch before the bidding starts.

Bigger group, better shout when your horse romps in. The club will help you sort it out on the day.
